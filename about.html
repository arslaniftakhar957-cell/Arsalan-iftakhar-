Our Story: Why We Started ScoreLab

In the modern digital world, content is king, but creating high-quality content consistently is extremely challenging. We observed that talented creators from around the world often struggle with three major problems: writer's block while writing video scripts, inability to find the right viral hashtags, and creating titles that actually get clicks. Premium AI tools exist, but they charge $20 to $50 per month, which is unaffordable for beginners, students, and creators from developing countries.

ScoreLab was founded to solve this inequality. Our founders, passionate about technology and content creation, decided to build a 100% free, no-signup, fast, and mobile-friendly toolkit that gives every creator – regardless of their country, language, or budget – access to professional-grade AI assistance. What started as a small project has now grown into a global resource used by creators worldwide.
