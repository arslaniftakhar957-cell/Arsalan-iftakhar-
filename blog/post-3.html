How to Write YouTube Titles That Get Clicks in 2026 (7 Formulas)

Published: Sep 10, 2026 | By Arslan Iftakhar

My video had great content but only 1.8% click-through rate. I changed just the title, same thumbnail, same video. CTR jumped to 6.4% and views went from 500 to 15,000 in a week.

Title is 80% of the game in 2026. Here are the 7 formulas I use daily.

1. The Number + Pain Formula

Bad: "Tips for YouTube growth"
Good: "5 Mistakes Killing Your Views (Fix #3 Today)"

Numbers catch eyes. Pain makes them click. I use odd numbers: 3, 5, 7, 9 work best.

2. The "I Tried" Formula

"I Posted Daily for 30 Days, Here's What Happened" - this got me 45K views. People love real experiments.

Even with 0 subs you can use it: "I Tried MrBeast's Title Strategy for 10 Videos"

3. The Year Hack

Adding 2026 increases clicks by 30% for tutorials. "YouTube SEO 2026" beats "YouTube SEO" because people want latest info.

I add year to every how-to video now.

4. Brackets = More Clicks

"How to Get Subs [Step by Step]" vs "How to Get Subs". The bracket version got me 2x CTR in test.

Use [2026 Guide], [With Proof], [No Ads] in brackets.

5. Keep It Under 55 Characters

Long titles get cut on mobile. My best titles are 40-50 characters. Check on phone before publishing.

Bad (72 chars): "The Ultimate Complete Guide to Growing Your YouTube Channel From Scratch"
Good (44 chars): "Grow to 1K Subs in 45 Days (Full Guide)"

6. Use Power Words

My list that works: Secret, Proven, Hack, Mistake, Stop, Why, How I, Exposed, Free.

"Stop Doing This on YouTube" outperformed "YouTube Tips" by 4x.

7. Test 3 Titles Per Video

I write 3 titles for every video and pick best using our Title Generator. Then after 24 hours, if CTR is below 4%, I change title. YouTube allows it and it works.

One title change saved my flop video and made it my most viewed.

What NOT to Do

Don't use ALL CAPS. Don't use clickbait that lies - "I Made $1M" when you made $10 will kill your channel. Retention drops and YouTube stops pushing.

Promise in title = deliver in first 60 seconds. That's the rule.

Quick Checklist

Before publishing, ask: Is it under 55 chars? Does it have number or power word? Would *I* click it? If yes, post it.

Start using these today. Your next video could be the one that pops.
